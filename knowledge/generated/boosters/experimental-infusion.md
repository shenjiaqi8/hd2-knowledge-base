# Experimental Infusion

**分类：** 强化剂 (`boosters`) · **ID：** `experimental-infusion`

**Wiki 原始词条：** https://helldivers.wiki.gg/wiki/Experimental_Infusion

## 简介

Experimental Infusion is a Booster in Helldivers 2. When equipped, all Helldivers will use a special Stim, which increases the movement speed and reduces damage taken, in addition to the standard properties of a Stim.

## 效果

In addition to restoring health, stims temporarily increase movement speed and damage reduction.
