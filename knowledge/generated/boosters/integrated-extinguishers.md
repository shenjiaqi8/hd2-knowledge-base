# Integrated Extinguishers

**分类：** 强化剂 (`boosters`) · **ID：** `integrated-extinguishers`

**Wiki 原始词条：** https://helldivers.wiki.gg/wiki/Integrated_Extinguishers

## 简介

Integrated Extinguishers is a Booster in Helldivers 2. When equipped, it reduces the amount of time to extinguish flames when any Helldiver squad member is lit on fire.

## 效果

Equips Helldiver uniforms with form-fitting, automated, rapid-response fire suppression systems. Significantly reduces the time it takes to naturally extinguish flames without conducting ground-aided smothering manuevers.
