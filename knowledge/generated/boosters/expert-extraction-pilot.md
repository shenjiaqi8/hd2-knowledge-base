# Expert Extraction Pilot

**分类：** 强化剂 (`boosters`) · **ID：** `expert-extraction-pilot`

**Wiki 原始词条：** https://helldivers.wiki.gg/wiki/Expert_Extraction_Pilot

## 简介

Expert Extraction Pilot is a Booster in Helldivers 2. When equipped, the extraction timer will be decreased, allowing for the Helldivers' squad to extract from a mission at a quicker rate. This booster does not affect missions where the Extraction Shuttle is immediately called in and lands after a mission is completed.

## 效果

Lowers the time it takes for the extraction shuttle to reach the extraction beacon.
