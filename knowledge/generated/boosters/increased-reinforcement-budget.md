# Increased Reinforcement Budget

**分类：** 强化剂 (`boosters`) · **ID：** `increased-reinforcement-budget`

**Wiki 原始词条：** https://helldivers.wiki.gg/wiki/Increased_Reinforcement_Budget

## 简介

Increased Reinforcement Budget is a Booster in Helldivers 2. When equipped, the amount of reinforcements is increased, allowing for greater number of reinforcements to be called in greater succession.

## 效果

Increases the number of available reinforcements.
