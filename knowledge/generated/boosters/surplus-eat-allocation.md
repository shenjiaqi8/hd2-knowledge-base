# Surplus EAT Allocation

**分类：** 强化剂 (`boosters`) · **ID：** `surplus-eat-allocation`

**Wiki 原始词条：** https://helldivers.wiki.gg/wiki/Surplus_EAT_Allocation

## 简介

Surplus EAT Allocation is a Booster in Helldivers 2. When equipped, a Helldiver squad gets 2 free uses of the EAT-17 Expendable Anti-Tank support weapon per mission.

## 效果

Grants two free uses of the Expendable Anti-Tank (EAT) Stratagem per mission; enough to grant a full squad one single-use rocket launcher each, provided squad members employ the proper equipment distribution protocol.
