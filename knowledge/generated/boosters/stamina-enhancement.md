# Stamina Enhancement

**分类：** 强化剂 (`boosters`) · **ID：** `stamina-enhancement`

**Wiki 原始词条：** https://helldivers.wiki.gg/wiki/Stamina_Enhancement

## 简介

Stamina Enhancement is a Booster in Helldivers 2. When equipped, all Helldivers will have increased max stamina and stamina recovery, allowing for longer sprinting times, and more physically-taxing movements.

## 效果

Increases all Helldivers' stamina capacity and recovery.
