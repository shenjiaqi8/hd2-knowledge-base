# Sample Extricator

**分类：** 强化剂 (`boosters`) · **ID：** `sample-extricator`

**Wiki 原始词条：** https://helldivers.wiki.gg/wiki/Sample_Extricator

## 简介

Sample Extricator is a Booster in Helldivers 2. When equipped, all Large Enemies will have a chance of dropping Common samples upon death. The amount varies, but the amount dropped per mission is capped at 10.

## 效果

Large enemies now have a chance of dropping Samples on death. Capped at 10 drops per mission.
