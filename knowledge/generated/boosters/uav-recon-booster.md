# UAV Recon Booster

**分类：** 强化剂 (`boosters`) · **ID：** `uav-recon-booster`

**Wiki 原始词条：** https://helldivers.wiki.gg/wiki/UAV_Recon_Booster

## 简介

UAV Recon Booster is a Booster in Helldivers 2. When equipped, all Helldivers will have increased effective radar range, allowing for easier recon of terrain.

## 效果

Increases all Helldivers' effective radar range.
