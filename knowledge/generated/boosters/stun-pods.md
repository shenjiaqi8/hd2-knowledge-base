# Stun Pods

**分类：** 强化剂 (`boosters`) · **ID：** `stun-pods`

**Wiki 原始词条：** https://helldivers.wiki.gg/wiki/Stun_Pods

## 简介

Stun Pods is a Booster in Helldivers 2. When equipped, all Hellpods (manned or unmanned), will stun nearby enemies upon impact with the surface.

## 效果

Exposes Hellpod wiring upon surface contact, stunning any units in the vicinity of the drop site.
