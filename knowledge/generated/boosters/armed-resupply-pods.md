# Armed Resupply Pods

**分类：** 强化剂 (`boosters`) · **ID：** `armed-resupply-pods`

**Wiki 原始词条：** https://helldivers.wiki.gg/wiki/Armed_Resupply_Pods

## 简介

The Armed Resupply Pods are a Booster in Helldivers 2. When equipped, all Resupply Pods come mounted with an automated custom AR-23P Liberator Penetrator lasting until ran out of bullets or the pod itself getting demolished.

## 效果

Mounts a modified AR-23 Liberator to all resupply pods, allowing them to double as automatic turrets.
