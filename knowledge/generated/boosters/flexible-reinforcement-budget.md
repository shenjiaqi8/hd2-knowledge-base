# Flexible Reinforcement Budget

**分类：** 强化剂 (`boosters`) · **ID：** `flexible-reinforcement-budget`

**Wiki 原始词条：** https://helldivers.wiki.gg/wiki/Flexible_Reinforcement_Budget

## 简介

Flexible Reinforcement Budget is a Booster in Helldivers 2. When equipped, the timer to gain reinforcements will be reduced. This effect only activates when there are no more reinforcements readily available.

## 效果

Reduce time until new reinforcements are granted once they've been depleted.
