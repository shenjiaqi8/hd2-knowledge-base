# Vitality Enhancement

**分类：** 强化剂 (`boosters`) · **ID：** `vitality-enhancement`

**Wiki 原始词条：** https://helldivers.wiki.gg/wiki/Vitality_Enhancement

## 简介

Vitality Enhancement is a Booster in Helldivers 2. When equipped, all Helldivers will be more resistant to damage and injury.

## 效果

Provides minor damage reduction from all sources for all Helldivers.
