# Dead Sprint

**分类：** 强化剂 (`boosters`) · **ID：** `dead-sprint`

**Wiki 原始词条：** https://helldivers.wiki.gg/wiki/Dead_Sprint

## 简介

Dead Sprint is a Booster in Helldivers 2. When equipped, all Helldivers will be able to keep sprinting after they're exhausted, though it will eat though your HP.

## 效果

Allows Helldivers to keep sprinting even when they are out of stamina, draining their health instead.
