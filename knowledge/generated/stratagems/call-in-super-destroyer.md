# Call In Super Destroyer

**分类：** 战略配备 (`stratagems`) · **ID：** `call-in-super-destroyer`

## 简介

The Call In Super Destroyer Stratagem is a used to call in a Helldiver's Super Destroyer for 1 minute during Commando Missions. It is only available 10 seconds after the super destroyer has left after its initial minute of being on the map.
