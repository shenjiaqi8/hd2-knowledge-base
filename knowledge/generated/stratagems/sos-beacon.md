# SoS Beacon

**分类：** 战略配备 (`stratagems`) · **ID：** `sos-beacon`

**Wiki 原始词条：** https://helldivers.wiki.gg/wiki/SoS_Beacon

## 简介

The SoS Beacon is a standard-issue Stratagem which causes the mission to be displayed as a higher priority mission to other Helldivers on their Galactic War table. It can only be thrown by the host of the current lobby.
