## 敌人条目

## Terminids

### Scavenger

- Wiki：[Scavenger](https://helldivers.wiki.gg/wiki/Scavenger)

The Scavenger is the weakest and most common of the Terminids, using its claws to attack Helldivers at close range.

### Pouncer

- Wiki：[Pouncer](https://helldivers.wiki.gg/wiki/Pouncer)

The Pouncer is a small Terminid that can use its wings to quickly approach Helldivers.

### Warrior

- Wiki：[Warrior](https://helldivers.wiki.gg/wiki/Warrior)

The Warrior is a medium-sized Terminid that is sturdier than its smaller counterparts, suiting it better for close range combat.

### Hunter

- Wiki：[Hunter](https://helldivers.wiki.gg/wiki/Hunter)

The Hunter is a small-sized Terminid that can use its wings to flank and pounce on Helldivers.

### Hive Guard

- Wiki：[Hive Guard](https://helldivers.wiki.gg/wiki/Hive_Guard)

The Hive Guard is a medium-sized Terminid with frontal Medium armor. They are able to enter a crouching stance to protect their vulnerable body.

### Brood Commander

- Wiki：[Brood Commander](https://helldivers.wiki.gg/wiki/Brood_Commander)

The Brood Commander is a lightly armored, large Terminid that can summon a trio of Warriors to assist it in combat.

### Alpha Commander

- Wiki：[Alpha Commander](https://helldivers.wiki.gg/wiki/Alpha_Commander)

The Alpha Commander is a more durable Brood Commander. The trio of Alpha Warriors it summons are faster than their original counterparts.

### Stalker

- Wiki：[Stalker](https://helldivers.wiki.gg/wiki/Stalker)

The Stalker is a large Terminid with the ability to camouflage, and possesses tongues strong enough to knock Helldivers off their feet.

### Shrieker

- Wiki：[Shrieker](https://helldivers.wiki.gg/wiki/Shrieker)

The Shrieker is a small, flying Terminid that engages in hit-and-run tactics, diving in to attack Helldivers before flying back up to the skies.

### Bile Spitter

- Wiki：[Bile Spitter](https://helldivers.wiki.gg/wiki/Bile_Spitter)

The Bile Spitter is a small Terminid that is part of the Caustic Strain subfaction.

### Nursing Spewer

- Wiki：[Nursing Spewer](https://helldivers.wiki.gg/wiki/Nursing_Spewer)

The Nursing Spewer is a medium-sized Terminid that is capable of ranged attacks. Besides spewing their bile, they emit a thin spore cloud around them.

### Bile Spewer

- Wiki：[Bile Spewer](https://helldivers.wiki.gg/wiki/Bile_Spewer)

The Bile Spewer is a medium-sized Terminid that is part of the Caustic Strain subfaction, and is a variant of the Nursing Spewer that can spew a stream of acidic bile at Helldivers.

### Charger

- Wiki：[Charger](https://helldivers.wiki.gg/wiki/Charger)

The Charger is a large Terminid. Living up to their name, Chargers charge at Helldivers in an attempt to trample them.

### Charger Behemoth

- Wiki：[Charger Behemoth](https://helldivers.wiki.gg/wiki/Charger_Behemoth)

The Charger Behemoth is a large Terminid and a variant of the Charger that is more heavily armored.

### Impaler

- Wiki：[Impaler](https://helldivers.wiki.gg/wiki/Impaler)

The Impaler is a large Terminid. Impalers burrow their "head" of three bladed tentacles, causing them to emerge near Helldivers to impede and attack them.

### Bile Titan

- Wiki：[Bile Titan](https://helldivers.wiki.gg/wiki/Bile_Titan)

The Bile Titan is a massive Terminid that can spew large amounts of its acidic bile, and can use its hulking legs to instantly kill Helldivers

### Hive Lord

- Wiki：[Hive Lord](https://helldivers.wiki.gg/wiki/Hive_Lord)

The Hive Lord is a colossal, worm-like Terminid. Currently, it is the largest and most durable enemy in the game.

### Spore Charger

- Wiki：[Spore Charger](https://helldivers.wiki.gg/wiki/Spore_Charger)

The Spore Charger is a large Terminid that is part of the Caustic Strain subfaction, and is a variant of the Charger that emits a thick spore cloud around it, obscuring nearby objects similarly to a Spore Spewer.

### Rupture Charger

- Wiki：[Rupture Charger](https://helldivers.wiki.gg/wiki/Rupture_Charger)

The Rupture Charger is a large Terminid that is part of the Rupture Strain subfaction, and is a variant of the Charger.

### Dragonroach

- Wiki：[Dragonroach](https://helldivers.wiki.gg/wiki/Dragonroach)

The Dragonroach is a massive flying Terminid, described by the Helldivers 2 developers as a "big flying Bile Titan".

## Automatons

### Trooper

- Wiki：[Trooper](https://helldivers.wiki.gg/wiki/Trooper)

The Trooper is a light Automaton Infantry unit equipped with a Fusion SMG and Grenades. They have a variant known as the Raider, which behaves in the same manner.

### Raider

- Wiki：[Trooper](https://helldivers.wiki.gg/wiki/Trooper)

The Trooper is a light Automaton Infantry unit equipped with a Fusion SMG and Grenades. They have a variant known as the Raider, which behaves in the same manner.

### Commissar

- Wiki：[Commissar](https://helldivers.wiki.gg/wiki/Commissar)

The Commissar is a light Automaton that often leads Automaton patrols and is equipped with a Fusion Pistol and a Heat Blade.

### Berserker

- Wiki：[Berserker](https://helldivers.wiki.gg/wiki/Berserker)

The Berserker is a melee-oriented, medium Automaton equipped with Meatsaws welded to their arms.

### Devastator

- Wiki：[Devastator](https://helldivers.wiki.gg/wiki/Devastator)

The Devastator is a medium Automaton equipped with a double-barreled Fusion Assault Cannon and medium armor protecting its torso.

### Heavy Devastator

- Wiki：[Heavy Devastator](https://helldivers.wiki.gg/wiki/Heavy_Devastator)

The Heavy Devastator is a medium Automaton and a variant of the Devastator, equipped with a Fusion Repeater and a large ballistic shield.

### Rocket Devastator

- Wiki：[Rocket Devastator](https://helldivers.wiki.gg/wiki/Rocket_Devastator)

The Rocket Devastator is a medium Automaton and a variant of the Devastator, equipped with Rocket Pods and a double-barreled Fusion Assault Cannon.

### Scout Strider

- Wiki：[Scout Strider](https://helldivers.wiki.gg/wiki/Scout_Strider)

The Scout Strider is a light Automaton. A Trooper pilots the Scout Strider, which is armed with a Heavy Fusion Repeater.

### Reinforced Scout Strider

- Wiki：[Reinforced Scout Strider](https://helldivers.wiki.gg/wiki/Reinforced_Scout_Strider)

The Reinforced Scout Strider is an Automaton that is an upgraded, deadlier Scout Strider, protecting its Trooper pilot with additional back plating and enhancing its weaponry with four heavy rockets to accompany its heavy fusion repeater.

### Hulk

- Wiki：[Hulk (disambiguation)](https://helldivers.wiki.gg/wiki/Hulk_(disambiguation))

Hulk (disambiguation)s are large, heavily armored Automatons equipped with various heavy weaponry.

### Annihilator Tank

- Wiki：[Annihilator Tank](https://helldivers.wiki.gg/wiki/Annihilator_Tank)

The Annihilator Tank is a very large Automaton vehicle with a Fusion Battle Cannon, coaxial Fusion Cycler and hull-mounted Heavy Fusion Repeater.

### Shredder Tank

- Wiki：[Shredder Tank](https://helldivers.wiki.gg/wiki/Shredder_Tank)

The Shredder Tank is a very large Automaton vehicle and variant of the Annihilator Tank that has its main armament replaced with a Quad-linked, Heavy Fusion Cycler turret.

### Factory Strider

- Wiki：[Factory Strider](https://helldivers.wiki.gg/wiki/Factory_Strider)

The Factory Strider is a massive Automaton vehicle that periodically deploys four Devastators. Its armaments include a twin set of Fusion Gatling Guns and a Fusion Repeater Cannon that is capable of unloading a burst of three explosive projectiles.

### Gunship

- Wiki：[Gunship](https://helldivers.wiki.gg/wiki/Gunship)

The Gunship is a large, flying Automaton vehicle equipped with rockets and Heavy Fusion Cycler.

### Dropship

- Wiki：[Dropship](https://helldivers.wiki.gg/wiki/Dropship)

The Dropship is an Automaton transport aircraft with no weapons equipped. Its only purpose is to transport and drop in reinforcements.

### Jet Brigade

- Wiki：[Jet Brigade](https://helldivers.wiki.gg/wiki/Jet_Brigade)

The Jet Brigade (abbreviated as JB) is an Automaton subfaction. They are enhanced variants of various Automaton infantry, equipped with jump-packs that grant them increased mobility and aggressive capabilities.

### Cyborg Legion

- Wiki：[Cyborg Legion](https://helldivers.wiki.gg/wiki/Cyborg_Legion)

The Cyborg Legion are an Automaton sub-faction. They are descendants of the Cyborgs from the First Galactic War.

## Illuminate

### Voteless

- Wiki：[Voteless](https://helldivers.wiki.gg/wiki/Voteless)

The Voteless is an Illuminate unit. They are Super Earth citizens turned into mindless creatures that form hordes to overwhelm Helldivers at close range.

### Watcher

- Wiki：[Watcher](https://helldivers.wiki.gg/wiki/Watcher)

The Watcher is a flying Illuminate that scans the battlefield for threats. It is the only Illuminate enemy that can call in reinforcements.

### Overseer

- Wiki：[Overseer](https://helldivers.wiki.gg/wiki/Overseer)

The Overseer is a medium infantry Illuminate equipped with a Pathfinder Lightspear that acts as both a ranged and melee weapon. The Overseer additionally carries an energy shield on its left arm, which it will use to protect itself when not firing.

### Elevated Overseer

- Wiki：[Elevated Overseer](https://helldivers.wiki.gg/wiki/Elevated_Overseer)

The Elevated Overseer is a flying Illuminate unit equipped with the rapid burst-firing Plasma Storm Rifle and Plasma Detonator grenades.

### Crescent Overseer

- Wiki：[Crescent Overseer](https://helldivers.wiki.gg/wiki/Crescent_Overseer)

The Crescent Overseer is a medium Infantry Illuminate carrying a powerful arcing Plasma Bombard.

### Harvester

- Wiki：[Harvester](https://helldivers.wiki.gg/wiki/Harvester)

The Harvester is a large, mechanical tripod created by the Illuminate. It is equipped with a shield that will absorb incoming damage from many weapons and stratagems until it has been broken, and fires a powerful, continuous laser from its single eye-mounted Disintegration Beamer at long range or an arcing electrical shock fired from its Tesla Ripper at short range.

### Warp Ship

- Wiki：[Warp Ship](https://helldivers.wiki.gg/wiki/Warp_Ship)

The Warp Ship is an enemy emplacement and transport aircraft created by the Illuminate.

### Stingray

- Wiki：[Stingray](https://helldivers.wiki.gg/wiki/Stingray)

The Stingray is an Illuminate close air support aircraft in Helldivers 2. Such units will often fly over the map before they spot a target, where they will attempt to strafe said targets with their Plasma Destructors.

### Leviathan

- Wiki：[Leviathan](https://helldivers.wiki.gg/wiki/Leviathan)

The Leviathan is a Massive Illuminate warship with huge firepower and durability in Helldivers 2.

### Fleshmob

- Wiki：[Fleshmob](https://helldivers.wiki.gg/wiki/Fleshmob)

The Fleshmob is an Illuminate heavy unit, being a hulking monstrosity comprising several Voteless and other animals fused into each other.

### Obtruder

- Wiki：[Obtruder](https://helldivers.wiki.gg/wiki/Obtruder)

The Obtruder is a light, flying Illuminate enemy that is part of the Appropriators subfaction. It is a variant of the Watcher that trades its ability to call in reinforcements in exchange for overwhelming Helldivers through their numbers and plasma orbs.

### Gatekeeper

- Wiki：[Gatekeeper](https://helldivers.wiki.gg/wiki/Gatekeeper)

The Gatekeeper is an Illuminate-piloted construct that is part of the Appropriators subfaction. It is capable of engaging targets at a distance.

### Veracitor

- Wiki：[Veracitor](https://helldivers.wiki.gg/wiki/Veracitor)

The Veracitor is an Illuminate-piloted construct that is part of the Appropriators subfaction. It is capable of fighting at close quarters with large claw-like appendages.

### Crusher

- Wiki：[Crusher](https://helldivers.wiki.gg/wiki/Crusher)

The Crusher is an Illuminate heavy melee unit that can regenerate health quickly. They are part of the Vote Snatchers subfaction.
